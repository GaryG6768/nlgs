Hole 1

Score agreement

A hole is agreed only when all submitted values for that player/hole are the same. A disagreement is flagged and is not treated as an agreed score by this test.

What to test

  1. Player A enters scores for all four players on a hole and saves.
  2. Player B logs in on another phone and enters the same hole with one score deliberately different.
  3. Refresh the status on either phone. The disagreement should appear.
  4. Change the disputed score so both entries agree, then refresh. The warning should disappear.
  5. Move to another hole and repeat. Each scorer's submission remains separate.
